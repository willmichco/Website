ABOUT THE FIRM

Understand the problem.
Then choose the right path.

Most clients do not come to a lawyer to win an argument about the law. They come to resolve something that is affecting their property, their family or their business.

That is why our first task in every matter is to establish what the client actually needs to achieve. Only then does the choice between negotiation, mediation, litigation or administrative complaint become meaningful.

We set out both the favourable route and the risks involved — including when our advice is that a claim should not be brought. We do not guarantee outcomes; under the Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct of Vietnamese Lawyers, no lawyer in Vietnam lawfully may.

HOW WE WORK

Four principles, consistently applied

A lawyer who stays with your file

One lawyer takes responsibility for your matter from intake to completion, so you always know who to speak to.

Reasoned recommendations

Every option is presented with its legal basis, conditions, risks, cost and expected steps — not as a conclusion you are asked to accept on trust.

Defined scope and fees

Scope, milestones and fees are agreed in a written legal services contract before work begins, in accordance with Article 55 of the Law on Lawyers. Anything outside that scope is discussed before it is incurred.

Confidentiality and independence

Client information is protected under Article 25 of the Law on Lawyers and Rule 7 of the Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct of Vietnamese Lawyers. Conflicts of interest are checked before any matter is accepted.
